# Adrective.Ai — Trailer Scene Prompts

Copy-paste these directly into your AI video generator. Each scene includes
prompts optimized for **Runway Gen-3 Alpha** and **Kling 2.0**. Generate 2-3
variations of each scene and select the best take.

**Global negative prompt** (append to every generation):
> No text, no words, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no people smiling,
> no bright colors, no blue tones, no stock footage look, no lens flare
> overload, no fast camera movement.

## Color Grading Notes (Apply in Post)

Apply these adjustments uniformly across all scenes in DaVinci Resolve or
your grading tool:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Lift (Shadows) | Crush to near-black, keep detail in darkest 5% |
| Gamma (Midtones) | Pull down slightly — keep overall image dark |
| Gain (Highlights) | Controlled — whites should be soft, not blown |
| Saturation | Desaturate globally by 20-30%, then boost red channel only |
| Red Channel | Isolate and refine — reds should be deep crimson, never orange |
| Color Temperature | Cool/neutral — no warm tones except the red accents |
| Contrast | High — this trailer lives in the extremes of light and dark |
| Film Grain | Add subtle fine grain (2-5%) for texture and cinematic feel |
